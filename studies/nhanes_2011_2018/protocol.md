# Protocol — final self-contained specification

Version 2, October 10, 2026. The initial four-cycle protocol was written before the first pooled estimates. The self-contained prompt arrived after preliminary pooled work; those estimates were known when this revision was written. This revision is documented before fitting the final Study A definitions and matched Study B models, but it is **post-exploration**, not prospectively preregistered. Earlier exploration remains separate and is not evidence of exact Calixte methods. No definitions will be selected by closeness to benchmarks.

## Evidence and estimands

Only the paper facts explicitly supplied in CODEX_NHANES_2011_2018_SELF_CONTAINED_PROMPT.md are treated as verified: four cycles2011–2018, ages18–64, measured≥130/80, prior-diagnosis unawareness, routine-care exposure, survey Poisson, and external benchmarks. Exact BP averaging, clinical formula, exclusions, care collapse, missingness and inferential reference are unknown. The PDF is not needed to execute this approximate/independent reconstruction.

A: association of lacking an operational routine non-emergency care place with unawareness among examined adults18–64 with measured BP≥130/80. Primary BP is separate means of available first3 measurements; both means are required for A eligibility. No medication-only cases. Unawareness=BPQ0202, awareness=1; other responses missing. Primary no_routine_non_emergency_care=1 for HUQ0302 or reported usual/most-often care type3 (ER), 0 for HUQ0301/3 and type1,2,4,5. Uncertain type6/refusal/missing remains missing when HUQ0301/3; a no-place response is lack regardless of a skipped type follow-up. HUQ040 in G,HUQ041 later. An ER-coded usual/most-often place is not proof that it is the only place used. Multiple places with documented non-ER type qualify as access. This is our explicit operationalization, not verified Calixte recoding.

Primary reconstructed A adjustment: centered age per decade, sex, four-category social race/ethnicity, college-graduate status, continuous PIR, obesity≥30 and smoking never/former/current. Demographic/SES terms describe possible common causes of access and reported recognition; obesity/smoking can relate to screening and clinical risk but may also follow diagnosis. This is associational adjustment, not a sufficient causal set. Clinical covariates require BMX/SMQ; DIQ/MCQ/DPQ cached in preliminary work are not used by final primary models. No significance-based selection. A1 unadjusted, A2 demographics, A3 SES, A4 additionally clinical. Primary A1–A4 share A4 complete cases; stage-specific samples are sensitivities. Modified quasi-Poisson with log link yields PR; diagnose fitted means>1 and do not plot them as probabilities.

B: original reviewed primary estimand, examined ages20–64, first3 available BP means, threshold≥130/80 or **reported current** BPQ050A1 medication. Retain medication-confirmed cases without BP. Outcome awareness; exposure HIQ0112 versus1. No medication status is inferred from skipped responses in the extension. The historical skip-no rule is recorded separately solely to audit comparability: positive-case membership is unchanged because skip-no cannot create a positive case. Negative-status denominators can differ; no overall medication-inclusive national hypertension prevalence is claimed here.

B1 uninsured; B2 add age10 and sex; B3 add six-category race, four-category adult education and continuous PIR, matching original variables without cycle terms. Identical logistic/modified-Poisson covariates give OR/PR. Cycle fixed effects are a separately named sensitivity. Stage-specific samples are reported with common-B3 simpler-model comparisons. Primary standardization uses bounded B3 logistic predictions over its weighted complete-case covariate distribution, joint Taylor uncertainty and independent refitted JKn checks. Marginal and coefficient primary intervals use model residual df; probability intervals use logit transform. OR is not PR or a causal effect; common-sample OR changes may reflect noncollapsibility as well as covariate adjustment.

## Harmonization and design

Official CDC G,H,I,J source XPORT/codebooks, merged within cycle on uniqueSEQN, then appended. Normalize tiny decoded numeric zero, keep valid DBP0, fail for documented invalid paired readings; no BP imputation. Full positive-weight examined frame before domains. WTMEC8YR=WTMEC2YR/4, original SDMVSTRA/SDMVPSU with nest=TRUE; cycle namespaces also validated. No FPC, lonely-PSU fail, no subsample laboratory weights or prepandemic files. Software-derived model residual/full design df are recorded, not guessed to be60. Alternative full-design and normal calibrations are labeled sensitivities.

Race/education/income categories and question eligibility are checked against all codebooks. Adult education DMDEDUC2 is20+; for A ages18–19, known youth grades0–14/55/66 establish below college, while youth code15 (“more than high school”) is ambiguous and left missing. SMQ020/040 target20+ in G and18+ in H/I/J; do not call an ineligible younger participant a never-smoker. The original B age boundary avoids that structural covariate gap. Household/family-income alternative categories do not allocate broad≥20k income code12 to an invented upper group. Per-cycle missingness and actual cohort age support are disclosed.

## Sensitivities fixed before revised estimates

A: strict second/third means; available2–3; ages20–64; no-place only (1/3 access vs2 lack); single-place (1 access vs2/3 lack); excluding ER-coded persons rather than classifying as lack; no clinical covariates; adult-only education; grouped household income versusPIR; cycle adjustment. These are constructed ambiguity analyses, not guesses selected to reproduce the paper.

B: measured-only130/80 (first3), medication-inclusive140/90, measured-only23 on ages18–64 as a labeled alignment comparison, original20–64 measured23, BP-complete restriction, alternative readings2–3, known-pregnancy exclusion, omitPIR, quadratic age, categoricalPIR and cycle adjustment. Common-sample B1/B2 and supplementary missing-PIR response weighting (MAR-dependent, nuisance/outcome refit in JKn) address observed sample selection but do not verify MAR/MNAR.

Complete-case counts, weighted retention, per-cycle missingness, sparse/event categories, score/rank/convergence, influence/replicate diagnostics, Poisson>1 and weight dispersion are reported. No multiple-testing significance hunt. Benchmarks n5345,56.2%unaware,81.7%routine,PR1.20[1.12,1.29] are used only in reporting. Case-eligible, descriptive and adjusted-model n are always distinguished. Numerical proximity cannot establish exact replication.

## Validation, reporting and limits

R survey is authoritative; Python separately reconstructs data and calculates equivalent Taylor score covariance/descriptors for comparison. Independently rebuild R variables and every model mask from authentic sources. Re-execute J-only original-variable models in this extension and compare stored historical outputs. Test merge/coding/missingness/BP/weights/design/domain/fingerprints and original preservation. Validate model standardization by refitted R JKn, and propagate missing-response-model estimation inside replicates. Freeze originalSHA manifest before extension.

Generate separate flows, Tables1–3, benchmark/comparability ledger, aggregate figures,8–10-page-style manuscript, faculty summary andQA. All new code/metadata remain portable; raw files, person-level derived data, modelRDS,caches and local logs are gitignored. Original files are preserved. Cross-sectional timing, treatment-linked selection, single-visit BP, unknown published details, covariate missingness, sparse support and historical unit nonresponse limit interpretation. No novelty, exact replication, current-population, clinical diagnosis, institutional approval or personal-authorship claim is manufactured.
